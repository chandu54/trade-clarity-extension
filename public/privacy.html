Privacy Policy for TradeClarity.market

Effective Date: April 12, 2026

At TradeClarity.market, we believe that your trading data is your business. This Privacy Policy describes how our Chrome Extension handles information when you use our services.

1. Local-First Data Philosophy

TradeClarity.market is built on a "local-first" architecture. We do not maintain central servers that collect or store your individual trading setup data.

  • Storage: All your stock lists, parameters, tags, and personal notes are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local.
  • No Tracking: We do not track your browsing history, your personal identity, or your individual trading performance.

2. Information We Access

To provide professional research features, the extension requires access to the following third-party sources:

  • Yahoo Finance: We fetch public market data (price, volume) to calculate metrics like ADR. No user-identifiable data is shared with Yahoo.
  • Google Gemini AI: Sectoral data (symbols and industry names only) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate insights. No personal notes or identifying data are ever shared.

3. Permissions Justification

  • Storage: Required to save your trading weeks and settings persistently.
  • Host Permissions: Required to securely fetch financial data from external sources.
  • ActiveTab: Used only when you are interacting with supported charting sites like TradingView.

4. Data Control

You have absolute control over your data. You can export your entire database as a JSON file or perform a "Full Reset" via the settings at any time to permanently wipe all local data.
