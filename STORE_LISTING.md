## 2. Long Description
**Master your swing trading routine with TradeClarity.market.**

TradeClarity is not just a stock list; it is a **process enforcer**. Designed for disciplined traders who follow methodologies like SEPA (Mark Minervini) or CANSLIM (William O'Neil), this extension helps you move from emotional decisions to data-driven execution.

### Key Features:
- **Weekly Workflow:** Organize your research into clean weekly containers to prevent stale watchlists.
- **Metric Quantification:** Define your own parameters (ADR, Relative Strength, RSI) and use our "Check" system to give every stock a quality score (e.g., 5/5).
- **AI Phenomena Research:** Leverage Google Gemini to perform deep-dive research into entire sectors, identifying leadership clusters and institutional entry triggers.
- **Live Syncing:** Automatically fetch real-time ADR and Liquidity metrics from Yahoo Finance as you build your list.
- **Interactive Analytics:** Visualize your risk concentration by sector, market cap, or custom tags with professionally designed charts.
- **TradingView Widget:** Log notes and manage your setups directly from TradingView charts without switching tabs.

**Transform your chaotic watchlists into a professional-grade research journal. Start your TradeClarity routine today.**
